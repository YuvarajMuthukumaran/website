01 Tokenize

Text isn't read.
It's chopped.

02 Attend

Meaning is a
weighted vote.

To make sense of "it", a model looks back over everything before it and decides what matters. Hover, tap or tab through the words to see where each one looks.

03 Predict

The answer is
a dice roll.

A model never "decides" a word. It scores every token it knows, turns the scores into odds, and samples. Temperature decides how adventurous that roll is.

    —top pick
    —entropy (uncertainty)

    Click any bar to choose that token yourself. Low temperature piles the odds onto the favourite; high temperature lets long shots through. The odds here come from a tiny hand-written table, but the maths — softmax over scores — is the real thing.

    04 Principles

    What you just saw.

    i.

    Numbers, not words

    Tokens become vectors — long lists of numbers where similar meanings sit close together. Everything after that is arithmetic.

    ii.

    Context is a vote

    Attention lets every token ask which earlier tokens matter. Each layer and each head asks a different question.

    iii.

    Output is odds

    The model outputs probabilities for every token in its vocabulary. A sampler — and a temperature — turn those odds into a word.
